# Functions

MUFY Unit 1 — the function families and techniques covered before Introductory Calculus. Notes are ordered roughly as they build on each other, though each one stands alone.

## Notes

1. [[Function Notation, Domain and Range]] — function notation $f(x)$, the vertical line test, domain and range, set/interval notation
2. [[Linear Functions]] — gradient-intercept form, finding the equation of a line, sketching via intercepts
3. [[Quadratic Functions]] — the three forms of a parabola, completing the square, the discriminant and quadratic formula, transformations, quadratic equations/inequalities
4. [[Polynomial Division and the Factor Theorem]] — long division of polynomials, the Remainder Theorem, the Factor Theorem
5. [[Cubic Functions]] — general/vertex/factored forms, factorising and solving a cubic, repeated factors
6. [[Reciprocal Functions]] — the hyperbola $y=\dfrac{a}{x-h}+k$, vertical/horizontal asymptotes
7. [[Square Root Function]] — endpoint, domain/range, reflections and transformations
8. [[Inverse Functions]] — finding $f^{-1}(x)$, the horizontal line test, reflection in $y=x$
9. [[Logarithmic Functions]] — the four log laws, solving log and exponential equations, change of base
10. [[Trigonometric Functions]] — the unit circle, exact values, reduction formulas, solving trig equations, sinusoidal graphs
11. [[Binomial Theorem]] — Pascal's triangle, expanding $(x+a)^n$
12. [[Functions - Practice and Revision]] — mixed worked problems spanning every topic above

## Formula Sheet

**Lines and polynomials**

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Gradient between two points | $m = \dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$ |
| Quadratic formula | $x = \dfrac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$ |
| Discriminant | $\Delta = b^2-4ac$ ($\Delta>0$: 2 roots, $\Delta=0$: 1 repeated root, $\Delta<0$: 0 real roots) |
| Turning point of $y=a(x-h)^2+k$ | $(h,k)$ |
| Remainder Theorem | Dividing $P(x)$ by $(x-a)$ leaves remainder $P(a)$ |
| Factor Theorem | $(x-a)$ is a factor of $P(x)$ $\iff$ $P(a)=0$ |

**Exponentials, logs and the binomial theorem**

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Log is the inverse of exponential | $y=\log_a x \iff a^y=x$ |
| Product law | $\log_a(mn) = \log_a m + \log_a n$ |
| Quotient law | $\log_a\!\left(\dfrac{m}{n}\right) = \log_a m - \log_a n$ |
| Power law | $\log_a(m^k) = k\log_a m$ |
| Change of base | $\log_a b = \dfrac{\log_c b}{\log_c a}$ |
| Binomial theorem | $(x+a)^n = \displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n}\binom{n}{k}x^{n-k}a^{k}$ |

**Trigonometry**

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Unit circle point at angle $\theta$ | $(\cos\theta, \sin\theta)$ |
| Pythagorean identity | $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta = 1$ |
| Tangent identity | $\tan\theta = \dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}$ |
| Negative-angle (odd/even) rules | $\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta,\ \cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta,\ \tan(-\theta)=-\tan\theta$ |
| ASTC rule | **A**ll positive in Q1, **S**ine in Q2, **T**angent in Q3, **C**osine in Q4 |

**Inverses and transformations**

| Concept | Formula |
|---|---|
| Inverse function | Swap $x$ and $y$ in $y=f(x)$, then solve for $y$ |
| Graph of $f^{-1}$ | Reflection of $y=f(x)$ in the line $y=x$ |
| Vertex/endpoint form (parabola, square root, hyperbola) | $a(x-h)^{\,\cdot}+k$ — $(h,k)$ shifts the graph, $a$ scales/reflects it |

> [!warning] Most common mistakes across these notes
> Dropping a factor when simplifying inside a square root or a log before reading off a domain/asymptote (see the corrections flagged in [[Functions - Practice and Revision]]); forgetting that the quadratic formula's $\pm$ gives **two** roots, not one; and mixing up which way a parabola/cubic/hyperbola is reflected or shifted when reading transformation constants off the wrong form.
